What is Orthodukso?

Orthodukso™ is a collaborative effort of a national network of Orthodox Christian laity.

Our Five Aims

  1. To direct inquirers to Orthodox theology and spirituality.
  2. To engage the web with Orthodox Christian teaching.
  3. To provide Christian world-view commentary.
  4. To facilitate exchange of Orthodox ideas.
  5. To develop a national Orthodox network.
